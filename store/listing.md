## Store listing tab

**Name**

Arth

**Summary** (the manifest `description`; 111 of 132 characters; already in the package)

Select a word or phrase in a ChatGPT conversation and get a short explanation of what it means in that context.

**Detailed description**

Understand what you're reading.

Arth is a companion for reading ChatGPT conversations: it explains a word or phrase in the context where it appears. While you read a conversation on chatgpt.com, select any word or short phrase and click the small "Arth" button that appears next to it. A few seconds later a short explanation appears beside your selection, written for that passage, not a generic dictionary definition.

How it works
• Select a word or phrase (up to 300 characters) inside a message in a ChatGPT conversation.
• Click "Arth".
• Arth sends your selection and a little of the surrounding conversation to ChatGPT in a separate background tab, using the ChatGPT session already open in your browser.
• The answer appears in a small card next to your selection. Press Escape or click elsewhere to close it.

What it is good for
• Technical terms, acronyms and jargon in a long answer
• A phrase whose meaning depends on the sentence around it
• Code identifiers and snippets
• Words in another language

Things to know before you install
• Arth works only on chatgpt.com, and only on text inside conversation messages. It does nothing on other websites.
• Arth has no AI of its own. Explanations are generated by ChatGPT, through the ChatGPT website in your own browser. ChatGPT's own terms, limits and data handling apply to those requests, and each explanation uses one message of whatever allowance your ChatGPT session has.
• To get an answer, Arth opens one extra ChatGPT tab in the background and reuses it. When you are signed in to ChatGPT, that tab uses ChatGPT's Temporary Chat mode.
• Arth has no account, no settings, no analytics and no ads. It does not send anything to its developer.
• Explanations usually take a few seconds, and around 10–15 seconds when you are signed in to ChatGPT.

Arth is an independent project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or produced by OpenAI. "ChatGPT" is a trademark of OpenAI and is used here only to describe the website Arth works with.

## Privacy practices tab

**Single purpose**

Arth explains a word or phrase that the user selects in a ChatGPT conversation, in the context of that conversation.

**Permission justifications**

| Item | Justification |
|---|---|
| `storage` | Used only for `chrome.storage.session`, to remember the ID of the one background ChatGPT tab Arth opened, so the same tab is reused if the extension's service worker restarts. No user content is stored. The value is cleared when the browser closes. |
| Host access to `https://chatgpt.com/*` (content scripts) | Arth's only function runs on this site. The content scripts (1) detect a text selection inside a conversation message and show the Arth button, (2) read the selected text and a bounded amount of nearby conversation text when the user clicks, and (3) in the one background tab Arth opens, type that request into ChatGPT's own message box and read back the reply. No other site is accessed. |
| Script in the page's main world (`src/frames.js`) | Runs only in the background tab Arth itself opened, never in the tab the user is reading. ChatGPT draws its replies using animation frames, which Chrome does not run in a background tab, so replies never appear there. This script runs those frame callbacks from a timer while that tab is in the background. It reads no page data and sends nothing anywhere. |

**Remote code**

No. All code is in the package. Arth does not load or evaluate any remote script, and makes no network requests of its own.

**Data usage** (suggested answers; please confirm they match your own reading of the categories)

- Website content: **Yes.** Text of the ChatGPT conversation the user is reading (the selection and nearby text), only when the user clicks Arth.
- Personal communications: **Yes, to be conservative.** A ChatGPT conversation can be regarded as a chat message history.
- Personally identifiable information, health, financial and payment, authentication, location, web history, user activity: **No.** Arth does not collect these. Note that conversation text can incidentally contain anything the user wrote there.

Certifications to tick:
- I do not sell or transfer user data to third parties, outside of the approved use cases.
- I do not use or transfer user data for purposes that are unrelated to my item's single purpose.
- I do not use or transfer user data to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes.

The one transfer that does happen, and should be stated plainly: the selected text and nearby conversation text are submitted to ChatGPT (chatgpt.com, operated by OpenAI) to produce the explanation. That is the extension's single purpose and it happens only on the user's click.

## Third-party service disclosure

Arth relies on one third-party service: the ChatGPT website (chatgpt.com), operated by OpenAI. Arth does not use the OpenAI API and has no server of its own. Requests are made by operating the ChatGPT web page in the user's own browser, under the user's own ChatGPT session (or as an anonymous visitor if the user is not signed in). Content submitted this way is handled by OpenAI under its own terms and privacy policy.

## Test instructions for reviewers

No account or credentials are needed.

1. Open https://chatgpt.com and send any question, for example "Explain inflation in 100 words."
2. In the reply, double-click a word (or drag across a short phrase).
3. A small "Arth" button appears just below the selection. Click it.
4. "Thinking…" is shown, then a short explanation. A second ChatGPT tab opens in the background; that is where the explanation is generated.
5. Press Escape or click elsewhere to dismiss.

Arth does nothing on any site other than chatgpt.com.

## Submission steps (in order)

Steps 1 to 3 and step 9 need your own account, payment and approval. Nothing here has been done on your behalf.

1. **Developer account.** Sign in at https://chrome.google.com/webstore/devconsole with the Google account that will own Arth. Accept the developer agreement and pay the one-time registration fee. The account's email cannot be changed later.
2. **Account settings.** Turn on 2-step verification for that Google account. In the dashboard's Account page, set the publisher name and verify the contact email.
3. **Privacy policy.** Put the text of `privacy-policy.md` on a public web page. Fill in the effective date, your publisher name and your contact email first.
4. **Upload.** In the dashboard choose "New item" and upload `dist/arth-0.1.0.zip`.
5. **Store listing tab.** Paste the detailed description, choose the category and language, and upload `icons/icon128.png` as the store icon, the screenshots from `store/screenshots/` in the order given in the screenshot plan, and `store/promo-small-440x280.png` as the small promo tile. Enter your support email.
6. **Privacy practices tab.** Paste the single purpose, the three justifications and the remote-code answer. Tick the data-usage boxes and the three certifications as drafted. Enter the privacy policy URL from step 3.
7. **Distribution tab.** Free. Choose visibility. "Unlisted" or "Private" (trusted testers) lets you run a real store install before going public.
8. **Test instructions tab.** Paste the reviewer instructions. No credentials are needed.
9. **Submit for review.** To keep control of the release moment, untick "Publish automatically after it has passed review"; you then have 30 days to publish after approval.

Before step 9, confirm for yourself that distributing an extension which operates the ChatGPT website on the user's behalf is acceptable under OpenAI's terms of use.
